About Aman Tailor

Aman Tailor is a ladies tailor in Amritsar, run by the same family since 1925. Started by Sardar Achhar Singh, continued by his son Sardar Pritam Singh and today run by Amandeep Singh, the shop has spent three generations on one thing — garments that fit properly and finish cleanly.

Located in the Joshipura and Amarkot area near Khalsa College for Women, we handle professional stitching, alterations and personalised ladies clothing with attention to comfortable fitting and individual styling requirements.

Our customers are not only from Amritsar. Families visiting from other cities in India and from Australia, Canada, the USA, England and New Zealand regularly get stitched with us, and we courier completed orders to addresses across the world.

For suit stitching, blouse or kurti fitting, alterations or other ladies tailoring requirements, call or WhatsApp to discuss your needs before visiting.

Our heritage

Three Generations. One Family. Since 1925.

Aman Tailor was never built on advertising. It was built on a hundred years of garments that fitted properly, handed down from father to son along with the shop itself.

  1. First generation 1925

    Sardar Achhar Singh

    Aman Tailor opened its doors in 1925, when Sardar Achhar Singh began stitching for families across Amritsar — every garment measured, cut and sewn by hand. The standard he set, an honest fit and a clean finish, is still the standard the shop works to.

  2. Second generation Carried forward

    Sardar Pritam Singh

    His son, Sardar Pritam Singh, took the craft forward and grew the shop's reputation in ladies tailoring. Many families in Amritsar who came to him for their weddings still come to us today, a generation later.

  3. Third generation Today

    Amandeep Singh

    The shop is now run by Amandeep Singh, son of Pritam Singh. He brings contemporary designs, modern styling and worldwide delivery to a craft that has stayed in the same family, in the same city, for a hundred years.

One family, one city, one craft — since 1925.
